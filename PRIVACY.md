# HAULIX Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 – valid from HAULIX 0.0.9 BETA

This policy explains which data HAULIX processes – today in the offline program, and later in the optional
HAULIX online services. It is part of the RyanTMP Software License Agreement (Schedule 1 – HAULIX).

**Responsible (controller):** RyanTMP – *[full name and postal address must be added here before the online
services start, as required by Art. 13 GDPR]* · Contact: https://github.com/RyanTMP/Haulix/issues ·
https://www.haulix-logging.com

## 1. The HAULIX program (offline)

- **Everything stays on your PC.** HAULIX reads telemetry from Euro Truck Simulator 2 and your save games and
  stores your logbook, statistics, settings and backups in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Haulix`. This data is not sent to
  the author or anyone else.
- **Update check.** When enabled, HAULIX asks the public GitHub releases of HAULIX (GitHub Inc., USA) whether a
  new version exists, on start and every 6 hours. Like every web request, this transmits your IP address and
  basic technical data to GitHub; no HAULIX data is sent. You can turn the check off in Settings → About.
  GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- **Natural voices.** If you download a voice, it is fetched once from GitHub and Hugging Face (USA). After that
  voices run offline.
- **Installer.** If the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is missing, the setup downloads it from Microsoft.
- **No tracking.** HAULIX contains no analytics, ads or tracking.
- **Deleting your data.** Uninstall HAULIX and choose to remove your data, or delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Haulix`.

## 2. HAULIX online services (optional, not active yet)

Online features – accounts, VTCs, company pages, events and convoys, job board, leaderboards and cloud sync –
are being prepared. **Nothing is sent until you sign in and turn a feature on.** When they start, the
following will apply:

| Data | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Account ID, display name and avatar from the service you sign in with (e.g. Discord, Steam) | Your HAULIX account | Art. 6 (1) b – providing the service you request |
| VTC membership, role, company pages, events, jobs and messages you create | VTC features | Art. 6 (1) b |
| Deliveries you choose to share (route cities, cargo, distance, income, score) | Leaderboards, company statistics, cloud sync | Art. 6 (1) b; for sharing with others Art. 6 (1) a (your choice) |
| IP address, time and technical logs | Security, abuse prevention, error analysis; logs are deleted after at most 14 days | Art. 6 (1) f – legitimate interest in a secure service |

- **No passwords.** HAULIX never sees your Discord or Steam password; sign-in uses OAuth. HAULIX only stores a
  sign-in token, protected by Windows on your PC.
- **Visibility.** What others can see (for example your name on a leaderboard or your company page) is shown in
  HAULIX before you share it. Private data stays private.
- **Hosting.** The online service will be hosted by a provider in the EU where possible. Any processor will be
  bound by a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Transfers outside the EU only with suitable safeguards.
- **Retention.** Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is
  deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires longer storage.
- **No sale.** Your data is never sold or used for advertising.
- **Age.** Online services are for users aged 16 or older, or with permission from a parent or guardian.

## 3. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), correction (Art. 16), deletion (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data
portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). Consent can be withdrawn at any time for the future. You also
have the right to complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77), for example the authority in your
country or state.

## 4. Changes

This policy will be updated before the online services start and whenever data processing changes. Important
changes are shown in HAULIX and in the changelog.
