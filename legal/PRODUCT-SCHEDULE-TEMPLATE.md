# Product schedule template

Paste this at the end of the RyanTMP Software License Agreement for a new program and fill in every line.
Delete lines that do not apply.

```
SCHEDULE <n> – <PRODUCT NAME>

Product:          <name> – <one-line description> (<platform>, its installer and updates)
Price:            Free. | One-time purchase of <price>. | Subscription: <price> per <month/year>, cancellable
                  at any time. | Free with optional Paid Features: <list>.
Allowed use:      Personal, non-commercial use as in section 1. | Commercial use allowed for <who/what>.
Devices:          Up to <n> devices per user / licence key. (only for paid products)
Online Services:  None. | Required for <features>. | Optional: <features>. Part B applies.
Game / platform:  Works with <game or service> by <owner>; not affiliated with or endorsed by <owner>.
Third-party rules: <features that may break a game's or server's rules, and that they are off by default>.
Add-ons / API:    <what may be extended and where the developer rules are>, or "Not offered".
Age:              Online features from 16 (or with guardian permission). | <other age rule>.
Privacy:          <Product> Privacy Policy (PRIVACY).
Third-party code: THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.
Special terms:    <anything that differs from the main agreement for this product>.
```
